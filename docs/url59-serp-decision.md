## Human need
A relative suspects or knows that someone close has an addiction and needs to know what they can do now, including when the affected person denies the problem or does not want treatment. The family member needs orientation without being turned into a diagnostician, therapist, police officer or financier of the addiction.

## Search-intent gap
The intent is broader than “how to convince an addict to enter rehab”. A useful national guide must cover: how to speak without diagnosing; how the family can seek professional orientation for itself; boundaries around money/debts/cover-ups; safety escalation; minors; violence; acute intoxication/medical emergency; gambling/behavioural addictions; and public-system entry points.

The page must not compete as a treatment-centre landing page. Its differentiator is public-first, non-commercial family navigation.

## Cannibalisation
No current sitemap URL has addiction-family support as its primary intent. Existing `/familia/` pages address estrangement, boundaries, emotional rejection, cohabitation distress and school bullying. Money pages cover debt but not addiction. Crisis/suicide pages remain separate safety routes.

## Editorial decision
Build URL59 as a family-support guide. Do not publish until a dedicated Safety Gate, Engineering Quality Gate and SEO Integrity all pass. No treatment-provider lead form, affiliate CTA or commercial rehabilitation recommendation.
