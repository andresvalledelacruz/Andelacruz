# URL59 — evidence note

Date checked: 2026-08-29
Scope: family support when a relative has a substance-use or behavioural addiction.

## Primary public sources

1. **Delegación del Gobierno para el Plan Nacional sobre Drogas (PNSD), Protocolo de Atención a Familiares.** The protocol explicitly evaluates the impact of addiction on the family, what relatives have already tried, changes in their daily life, their knowledge of addiction, and the role/limits of family participation in treatment. It also treats the affected person's consent and choice about which relative participates as relevant.
   - https://pnsd.sanidad.gob.es/profesionales/publicaciones/catalogo/bibliotecaDigital/publicaciones/pdf/ProtocoloAtencionaFamiliares.pdf

2. **Ministerio de Sanidad — Centros de Atención a las Adicciones.** National public map/search interface by autonomous community, province and address/city/postcode. This is the preferred national navigation resource rather than selecting private centres.
   - https://mapas.sanidad.gob.es/mapascon/vermapa.jsf?idm=1

3. **Ministerio de Sanidad — Localiza Salud.** Current records show public/free addiction resources and examples that explicitly include attention to families, supporting the statement that relatives can look for specialised public resources rather than having to solve the situation alone.
   - https://localizasalud.sanidad.gob.es/maparecursos/main/ResourcesSearchDetail.action?id=1652
   - https://localizasalud.sanidad.gob.es/maparecursos/main/ResourcesSearchDetail.action?id=11628

## Safety/editorial constraints derived from evidence

- Do not diagnose addiction from a checklist or from a relative's description.
- Do not promise that confrontation, ultimatums or a specific family technique will make someone accept treatment.
- Distinguish supporting a person from assuming responsibility for their treatment.
- Respect the affected adult's autonomy and confidentiality; family involvement in treatment is not unlimited.
- A family member may seek orientation/support for their own situation even when the affected person is not ready to engage.
- Route acute medical danger to emergency services and bridge suicide risk to the site's dedicated suicide-support pathway/024/112 as appropriate.
- Route violence/coercion to safety-first domestic/sexual violence resources; never recommend family mediation during active abuse.
- For minors, safeguarding and professional assessment override secrecy.
- No commercial rehab funnel, lead capture or affiliate recommendation on this high-risk page.

## Freshness
The Ministry's national addiction-centre map and Localiza Salud records were accessible and current when checked on 2026-08-29. Older PNSD family protocols are used for stable family-care principles, not for time-sensitive service details.
