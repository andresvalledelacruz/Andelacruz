# Copyright & Intellectual Property Policy — Desgracias.es

Status: mandatory publication policy.

## Principle

Desgracias.es must not publish material merely because it is useful or available online. Copyright/IP compliance is a release requirement. When rights, licence, provenance or the legal basis for reuse are uncertain, the default decision is **HOLD / NO-GO** until the uncertainty is resolved.

This policy is deliberately more conservative than trying to operate at the legal boundary. It is an engineering/editorial control, not individual legal advice.

## Spanish legal baseline

The primary reference is the consolidated Texto Refundido de la Ley de Propiedad Intelectual (Real Decreto Legislativo 1/1996) and current official guidance from the Ministerio de Cultura.

Operational consequences:

1. Original literary, artistic and scientific expression is protected; publication on the Internet does not make it free to reuse.
2. There is no project rule such as “one paragraph is always allowed”. Any quotation exception must be assessed under the applicable legal conditions and purpose; source and author attribution do not by themselves legalise otherwise unauthorised copying.
3. Translation, adaptation, revision, compendium, summary and extract can themselves implicate rights in the underlying work. Cosmetic paraphrasing is therefore prohibited as a workaround.
4. Ideas, methods, concepts and facts may be researched and learned from, but Desgracias.es must create its own expression, structure, synthesis and added value.
5. Public-domain and openly licensed works may be reused only after verifying the relevant status/licence and complying with its conditions, including attribution where required.
6. Photographs, illustrations, graphics, tables, maps, audio, video, screenshots, book covers and other third-party assets require the same provenance/licence discipline as text.

Official references:
- BOE, RDL 1/1996: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1996-8930
- Ministerio de Cultura, Propiedad Intelectual: https://www.cultura.gob.es/cultura/derechos-culturales/propiedad-intelectual.html
- Ministerio de Cultura, autorización para utilizar obras: https://www.cultura.gob.es/cultura/propiedadintelectual/la-propiedad-intelectual/preguntas-mas-frecuentes/autorizacion-para-utilizar-obras.html

## Publication rules

### Default: original synthesis

Research may use multiple lawful sources. The published result must be independently structured and written for the concrete human need addressed by Desgracias.es. Do not preserve a source's distinctive sequence, examples, metaphors, headings or expressive choices merely by changing vocabulary.

### Verbatim quotations

A quotation is exceptional, not the default. Before publication record:
- work/title and author where applicable;
- source URL or bibliographic provenance;
- why verbatim wording is necessary rather than original synthesis;
- applicable permission/licence/legal basis;
- why the amount used is no more than justified for that purpose.

If that record cannot be completed, do not publish the quotation.

### Books and paid material

Never transcribe chapters, substantial passages, exercises, tables or distinctive frameworks from books, ebooks, paid courses, subscription material or private PDFs into the site. Never reconstruct a work through a sequence of individually short excerpts. Never use paraphrase to create a substitute for the original.

### Third-party visual/media assets

Do not copy an image or media asset from search results, social networks, publishers or arbitrary websites merely because it is publicly accessible. Store licence/provenance evidence for any third-party asset. Prefer Desgracias.es-owned assets, commissioned assets, verified public-domain material or licences that clearly permit the intended use.

### Official/public-sector sources

Official sources are preferred for factual/YMYL verification, but official availability does not automatically mean every embedded photograph, illustration or third-party element is unrestricted. Reuse only what is legally reusable and keep attribution/licence evidence where required.

## Copyright/IP Gate

Every editorial PR must answer:

- [ ] Is the prose independently written rather than copied or cosmetically paraphrased?
- [ ] Are any verbatim third-party passages necessary and legally justified?
- [ ] Are author/source and permission/licence/legal basis recorded for each quotation where applicable?
- [ ] Are books, PDFs, courses and paid/subscription sources used only for research unless reuse is clearly authorised?
- [ ] Are third-party images/media/assets covered by verified provenance and licence?
- [ ] Does the page avoid reconstructing the structure or expressive sequence of a protected work?
- [ ] If any answer is uncertain, is release held for legal review rather than assumed safe?

## Retrospective audit

Existing production content is in scope. Automated checks are only a first screen and cannot prove absence of infringement or plagiarism. High-risk pages must receive human/source-level review, prioritising:

1. pages known to have been developed from books, PDFs, courses or supplied third-party documents;
2. pages containing quotations, blockquotes, named books/authors or distinctive frameworks;
3. pages containing third-party images, diagrams, tables or screenshots;
4. long-form pages whose wording or structure closely follows one source.

Any questionable material is **HOLD**, rewritten from first principles where appropriate, or escalated as `Pendiente de Andrés / revisión jurídica` if legal judgement or permission is required.
